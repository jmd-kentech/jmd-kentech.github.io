Jung Materials Design Group

Welcome

We are a computational materials design group at the Korea Institute of Energy Technology (KENTECH).
Our research focuses on understanding and engineering the structure–property relationships of energy materials, with particular emphasis on halide perovskites and related materials for photovoltaics and light-emitting devices.
